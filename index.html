how to use

Load a recording, then look across the waveform and spectrogram from left to right.

  1. Check the detected BPM and bar lines in tempo & beat grid. You can edit the tempo, nudge the grid, or click the waveform and set that playhead position as Bar 1.
  2. Drag across the waveform to select a time range.
  3. Drag a box on the spectrogram to select time and frequency together. You can also enter the exact range in the annotation panel.
  4. Choose a category, write what you hear, then add the annotation.
  5. Use the play or loop symbols on a saved region to hear that exact area; spectrogram regions are frequency-isolated.
  6. Turn any selected time range into an arrangement section. Its edges snap to bar starts, then you can click its block to give it your own name.
  7. Turn on the metronome to hear the current beat grid. A higher ping marks beat 1 of each bar, and manual tempo or grid corrections take effect immediately.
  8. Use the spectrum band buttons, pitch-locked speed control, search, and optional read-ahead notes while listening.
  9. Press + on a note to link more regions. Drag each region or enter its exact range, then press + again to finish.
  10. Use focused view when you want the waveform and spectrogram to fill the window.
  11. Save or share an editable session. You can include a private audio-download link so the recipient can load the same recording.
