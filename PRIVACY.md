# Privacy Policy

**Music Analyser**

Effective date: 20 July 2026
Developer: Pat Carroll

This policy explains how Music Analyser handles information when you use it.

## Information the app handles

Music Analyser handles an audio file only after you choose or drop it into the app. It may read the file's common embedded music metadata, including artist, track title, album name, and artwork. The app also handles the annotations, arrangement markers, custom categories, rhythm-grid corrections, and editable artist or track details that you create.

To reopen a recent analysis in the desktop app, the app stores a local reference to the selected recording, including its path when Electron makes that available, file name, size, and last-modified time.

## How information is processed and stored

Audio decoding, waveform and spectrogram drawing, loudness and tempo analysis, beat-grid estimation, metronome playback, and annotation work happen on your device. The app does not include an account system, an app-operated upload service, cloud synchronisation, or a backend that receives recordings or notes.

The app stores analysis data and preferences in your device's local browser storage. This can include annotations, arrangement markers, categories, artist and track metadata, compact artwork thumbnails, recent-analysis references, corrected rhythm-grid settings, an optional audio-download link that you provide, and display or playback preferences such as the metronome setting. You can export an editable JSON session; it does not contain the audio recording itself. Clearing the app's stored data removes this locally saved app data, while exported files remain where you saved or shared them.

When you choose to copy analysis text, it is placed on your system clipboard. When you choose the system sharing option, the exported JSON session is passed to the sharing destination you choose; that destination's privacy practices then apply.

## Network use and third-party services

The app's interface requests the IBM Plex Mono font from Google Fonts when a network connection is available. Google may receive connection information, such as your IP address and browser or device details, under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Other than this font request and a sharing destination you explicitly choose, the current app code does not show an app-operated network service, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, authentication, payment processing, or cloud storage.

## Permissions

The app accesses a recording only after you select it. The desktop version can read the selected file's metadata and reopen a recent local recording reference. The current project does not declare or use camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photo-library access.

## Sharing, retention, and your choices

Music Analyser does not sell your recordings or annotations. Your locally stored data remains on your device until you clear the app's stored data or remove the relevant local files. Please keep your own backups of important analyses and exported sessions.

## Children's privacy

Music Analyser is not designed for children to provide personal information. The app does not include accounts or a direct collection process for personal information.

## Security

The app is designed to process recordings locally and uses Electron's isolated renderer configuration. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please keep your device and exported files appropriately protected.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new effective date. For privacy questions, contact info@pcarroll.au.
